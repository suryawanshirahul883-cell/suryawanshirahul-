👑 SOCIAL REFORMER AND VISIONARY KING

RAJARSHI SHAHU MAHARAJ

26 June 1874 – 6 May 1922

Rajarshi Shahu Maharaj was a great social reformer and the ruler of Kolhapur. He worked for education, equality, social justice and the upliftment of disadvantaged communities. His reforms created new opportunities for many people.
